# Product guardrails

## Consent

Both participants must receive a clear explanation and explicitly enable physiological reaction sharing. Consent can be withdrawn immediately without ending the chat.

## Age

The dating/reaction product is adults-only. Do not infer or display attraction-related physiological signals involving minors.

## Display restrictions

Do not display exact BPM to the other participant by default. Display broad, non-valenced trends with uncertainty and an “insufficient signal” state.

Forbidden labels include:

- likes you;
- dislikes you;
- attracted;
- aroused in a sexual sense;
- lying;
- afraid of you;
- compatible;
- trustworthy.

## Retention

Default: process transiently and discard. Research recording requires a separate consent flow, purpose limitation, retention period, and deletion mechanism.

## Fairness

Benchmark performance across skin-tone ranges, camera classes, illumination conditions, age ranges, facial hair, glasses, makeup, and movement. Publish abstention and failure rates, not only average error.

## Safety UX

- show when analysis is active;
- provide pause and revoke buttons;
- disclose uncertainty;
- avoid manipulative gamification;
- never encourage users to pressure another participant based on a reaction indicator;
- provide report/block tools independent of physiology.
